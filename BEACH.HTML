Discover the coastal scenery of Miagao

Discover the Coastal Beauty

Miagao is a coastal municipality in Iloilo. Its coastal communities offer sea views, local fishing activities, and opportunities to appreciate seaside life.

Activities to Enjoy

  • Enjoy the sea views.
  • Take photos of the shoreline.
  • Spend time with family and friends.
  • Learn about local fishing communities.

Protect Our Beaches

Keep the coast clean, avoid littering, and follow local safety rules. Check the location and accessibility of a beach before visiting.
