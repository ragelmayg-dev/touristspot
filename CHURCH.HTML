Discover Miagao Church

A historic landmark in Iloilo, Philippines

A Famous Historical Landmark

Miagao Church, officially known as the Santo Tomas de Villanueva Parish Church, is one of the most important historical landmarks in Miagao, Iloilo.

History

The church was completed in 1797. It forms part of the UNESCO World Heritage Site called the Baroque Churches of the Philippines.

Architecture

The church is famous for its massive stone structure and detailed facade, which reflects religious art and local cultural influences.

What to Appreciate

  • Historical architecture
  • Detailed stone carvings
  • Religious heritage
  • Local history and traditions
