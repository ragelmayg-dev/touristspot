Celebrating the Heritage of Miagao

Welcome to Miagao

Miagao is a municipality in Iloilo, Philippines. It is known for its historical landmarks, religious traditions, local customs, and community life.

Historical Heritage

Miagao Church, also known as the Santo Tomas de Villanueva Parish Church, is an important historical landmark. It was completed in 1797 and is part of the UNESCO World Heritage Site, Baroque Churches of the Philippines.

Local Food

Visitors can explore local eateries and enjoy Filipino and Ilonggo dishes. Supporting local food businesses helps the community.

Traditions and Celebrations

Religious activities and community celebrations help preserve the customs and traditions of Miagao. Check local announcements for event schedules.

Why Culture Matters

  • Preserves local history.
  • Promotes community identity.
  • Encourages tourism.
  • Supports local businesses.
